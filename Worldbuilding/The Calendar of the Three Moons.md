## Overview

The people of **Tenebris** follow the **Three Moon Cycle**, a **364-day year** shaped by the orbits of:

- **Azura** (28-day cycle)
- **Roja** (45-day cycle)
- **Plata** (85-day cycle)

The **13-month system** reflects the balance between the celestial forces, influencing magic, tides, seasons, and fate.

---

## Moon Phases

| Phase                    | Meaning                                        |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------- |
| **New (N)**              | Moon is dark, a time of secrecy.               |
| **Waxing Crescent (WC)** | Energy builds, preparations begin.             |
| **First Quarter (FQ)**   | Growth, ambition, and change.                  |
| **Waxing Gibbous(WG)**   | Anticipation, nearing climax.                  |
| **Full (F)**             | Power peaks, the time of clarity or chaos.     |
| **Waning Gibbous (NG)**  | Reflection, decline of influence.              |
| **Last Quarter (LQ)**    | Dissolution, truths revealed.                  |
| **Waning Crescent (NC)** | Endings, rest, and whispers of the next cycle. |

## Closing Thoughts

This celestial calendar **not only marks time but shapes fate**, influencing the tides, magic, rituals, and even the destiny of **Tenebris’ Riftborn adventurers**. The balance of the **Three Moons** is ever-changing—**those who understand their cycles will find power, while those who ignore them will be lost to the void.**
